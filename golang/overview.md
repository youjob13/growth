Go is compiled, strongly typed language.

Go is garbade collected language. Go includes a runtime to every executable binary (a little bit of extra code, that helps collect garbage). It consumpts much less memeory than JVM (Java Virtual Machine) 

## Why do we need Golang?

1) Execution Speed - Go executes faster than interpretive (not-complied) languages (JS, Python, Ruby PHP) 
2) Compilation Speed - Go complites faster than other compilated languages (Rust, C, C++, Java, C#)
